This agreement is adapted from the Apache Software Foundation's Individual
Contributor License Agreement, which is widely used and widely understood. It
grants a broad license rather than transferring ownership: **you keep the
copyright in everything you write.**

By signing, you agree to the terms below for all contributions you make to the
`coven` repository (formerly `chattersnow-web`), past and future.

**"You"** means the individual signing this agreement.

as described in `LICENSE`, and any successor entity to which the Core is
assigned.

**"Contribution"** means any work of authorship you intentionally submit for
inclusion in the repository — code, database migrations, documentation, design
assets, configuration, or anything else — submitted by pull request, commit,
issue attachment, or any other means. It does not include anything you
conspicuously mark "Not a Contribution."

## 2. You keep your copyright

You retain all right, title, and interest in your Contributions. This agreement
grants licenses; it does not transfer ownership.

## 3. Copyright license

You grant the Project Owner and recipients of software distributed by the
Project Owner a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable
copyright license to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display,
publicly perform, **sublicense**, and distribute your Contributions and any
derivative works of them.

The sublicensing right matters and is the reason this agreement exists: it is
what allows your Contribution to be included in the perpetual license granted to
Chatter Snow, Inc., in the codebase if it is ever assigned to that organization
or to a successor company, and in the product if it is ever licensed to other
nonprofits commercially. Without it, no such decision can include your code.

You grant the Project Owner and recipients of software distributed by the
Project Owner a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable
patent license to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and

If any entity institutes patent litigation alleging that the project or a
Contribution infringes a patent, the patent licenses granted under this
agreement to that entity terminate as of the date the litigation is filed.

## 5. Your representations

You represent that:

(a) each Contribution is your original creation, or you have the right to submit
it under this agreement;

(b) you are legally entitled to grant the licenses above — in particular, if
your employer has rights in intellectual property you create, you have received
permission to make the Contribution on their behalf, or your employer has waived
those rights, or your employer has signed a separate entity agreement;

(c) any third-party material in a Contribution is identified as such, with its
source and license stated, in the Contribution itself or in the pull request
description; and

(d) no Contribution contains any real personal data about any participant,
donor, volunteer, staff member, or other identifiable individual.

## 6. No warranty, no obligation

You provide your Contributions on an "AS IS" basis, without warranties of any
kind. Nothing in this agreement obligates the Project Owner to use, merge, or
retain any Contribution, or to compensate you for it. You are not entitled to
payment, equity, or any share of revenue by virtue of contributing.

## 7. Notice of changed facts

If any representation in Section 5 stops being accurate — you change employers,
or discover you lacked the rights you believed you had — tell the Project Owner
promptly.
